Daniel

My name is Daniel, but you can call me Danny if you'd like. I'm a software engineer with 3 years experience and counting working in big tech in the Bay Area. My experience has been mostly in robotics— started with spatial geometry and mapping/SLAM, now more AI integration and distributed systems. Android development has been a constant in my few years, running as the app & ui layer in the products i've worked on. For being a software engineer, I'm very very anti tech. I believe that our smartphones, social media, and being chronically online are all means of distraction and extraction of attention. I'm anti consumption, and I believe you should always choose the option that gives you independence. Learning to cook is one of them. You should always use a VPN, pay with cash, and be very weary of giving your personal information to websites.

I have a passion for free and open source software, driven in part by this desire for independence and in part by a belief that we should return to a sense of normalcy when using the internet, thinking back to the times when it was more about information and communication, and less about attention and value extraction.

I'm anti-nerd in that I believe everyone should try their best to be a well rounded human, and specializing in a domain doesn't serve as an excuse to not be capable in other things. Fitness is not a passion for me, but rather a requirement in that I believe most people should strive to be as strong and physically capable as possible. I also believe that you need some degree of physicality, as we as humans have not evolved nearly as much as technology might make you think we have, and physical ability still lives in our subconscious.

My days are simple: I wake up, I work out, I eat, sauna, shower, then work for the rest of the day. I believe success is derived from being good at the simple things and managing your time wisely.
